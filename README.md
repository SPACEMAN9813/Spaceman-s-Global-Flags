# Spaceman-s-Global-Flags
Spaceman’s Global Flags is a comprehensive collection of real-world national, regional, and historical flags, made for the use with the Conformal Decals mod in KSP. Whether you are recreating real missions, making an Alt-history, or just adding personality to your spacecraft, this mod gives you the tools to represent the world!

All flags included are sourced from public domain or equivalent free-use resources, ensuring they are safe to use and share within the community.

Features:

-Wide selection of real-world national flags
-Additional regional and historical variants where available
-Clean, high-resolution textures optimized for decals
-Fully compatible with Conformal Decals

Perfect for:

-RSS / RO playthroughs
-Real-world mission recreations
-Alternate history space programs
-Multiplayer or faction-based saves

Requirements:

-Conformal Decals
